ABOUT
-----
Mad Max and the revenge of the killer pumpkins, on the Commodore 64. It is
Halloween, the bones are buried in the garden and the pumpkins have come alive.
You are Mad Max, a very hungry beagle: dig up every bone in all fourteen
gardens before the pumpkins catch you. You have nine lives, one step per key
press, and four keys of your choosing (A Z N M by default). Keyboard only, no
joystick. Written in 6502 assembly language, with sprites, multicolor
graphics and SID music and sound effects. Requires a Commodore 64 or 64C (PAL),
or the VICE emulator (x64sc).

FILES
-----
MadMaxC64.d64 (1541 disk image; LOAD"*",8,1 then RUN, or attach it in VICE and
autostart); MadMax-C64-Manual.pdf (the player's manual: controls, the garden,
the fourteen gardens, lives and tips, and credits); source/ (reserved for the
source code, to be added).
