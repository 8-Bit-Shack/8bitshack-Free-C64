==============================================================================
GAME TITLE : Oubliette C64
GAME NAME  : oubliette_c64
FOLDER     : oubliette_c64
==============================================================================

ABOUT
-----
Escape a dungeon of seven cells, one careful step at a time. Each key press
moves you exactly one cell, there is no clock and nothing chases you; the only
danger is your own next step. Every level has a trick of its own: doors that
move you, keys, locks, hidden traps. This is the Commodore 64 version of Dan
Henderson's Oubliette, with the same seven levels, maps and rules as the
original. Written in 6502 assembly language, with multicolor graphics and SID
music and sound effects. Play with the cursor keys, W A S D or a joystick in
port 2. Requires a Commodore 64 or 64C (PAL), or the VICE emulator (x64sc).

FILES
-----
OublietteC64.d64 (1541 disk image; LOAD"*",8,1 then RUN, or attach it in VICE
and autostart); Oubliette-C64-Manual.pdf (the player's manual: controls, the
dungeon, level-by-level hints, tips and credits); source/ (reserved for the
source code, to be added).
